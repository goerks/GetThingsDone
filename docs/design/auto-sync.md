# Design: Automatic sync, feedback and sync log

Issue: [#30](https://github.com/goerks/getthingsdone/issues/30). Mockups (German, reviewed by the PO): [`auto-sync-variants.html`](auto-sync-variants.html). Screenshots: `auto-sync-*.png`. Status: proposal, not decided.

- Feedback after a sync: 1 toast (recommended), 2 status line next to the icon, 3 icon with tick and tooltip. Errors and open conflicts stay longer and have a button.
- Automatic sync: one switch (all open issues or none), rhythm A start/return, B plus every 15 minutes (recommended), C selectable; no background run with the app closed.
- Sync log in Settings → GitHub: one row per run, details per ticket and field, last 100 runs or 90 days.
